CHAPTER 01 — IN ACTION
A WORKING DEFINITION

Executive presence is the ability to inspire confidence — in your team, your peers, your customers — through how you show up, speak, and listen.

It is built — not born — across three pillars.

01
🦁 GRAVITAS
Confidence under pressure. Decisiveness. The signal you can be trusted when stakes are high.
02
🗣️ COMMUNICATION
Clarity of thought. Command of language. The discipline to read a room and adjust.
03
✨ APPEARANCE
Your energy. Your polish. The visual signals that reinforce — or undercut — everything you say.

CHAPTER 05

Preparation
101.

How seasoned leaders stay ahead — before they ever walk in the room.

HABITS OF READINESS

Four things to do before the room opens.

📚 READ EVERYTHING
Skim broadly. Read deeply on the topic. Then read the other side.
🎯 DEFINE YOUR GOALS
Name three outcomes you need by the end. Without goals, the conversation drifts.
💬 START WITH THE BASICS
Direct questions outperform clever ones. Let the question do the work.
🧠 IMAGINE THE DIALOGUE
Walk it through in your head. Anticipate pushback. Sequence your asks.
PREPARATION 101 — OBSERVER

The greatest observer in the room is the most powerful person in the room.

THE MASTERY OF READING PEOPLE
To observe people — to truly read them — is a mastery. When anyone speaks, ask yourself: but why? Why do you think that? Because understanding someone's point of view, their opinion, their approach — that's where influence begins.
✓
Some people you can reason with. Explain: "I think the team would appreciate it if you respected that we don't have to agree — let's just move forward."
✕
Others are brick walls. The definition of insanity is running into one over and over expecting a different result. Know the difference.
PROTECT YOUR VOICE
Your voice is your greatest asset. You have to protect it. You also have to know who is worthy of it.
Are you going to spend energy giving the best version of yourself to someone who doesn't appreciate it — or even want it?
Scan the room. Know who you're willing to give that energy to — and who will actually receive it the way you intend.
PREPARATION 101 — SLOW DOWN

If you really want to highlight a point — slow down.

RATE OF SPEECH AS A TOOL
Most of us are nervous. We have a default rate of speech. But changing your pace changes what your audience sees as important.
Without pacing:
"Right now I'm going to go through the five core foundations and take you through one to three speech that I told you about pitching ability and then after that the importance of the other three—"
Your audience has no idea what is important and what is not.
CLARITY IS THE GOAL
With pacing:
"I'm going to take you through... the core five... vocal foundations."
Now your audience has a point of focus. The biggest thing people want from communication is clarity.
If you are going to be clear, the delivery needs to be clear — so the receiver gets what you intend.
It's not just the exchange of information. How you say something impacts how they receive it.

ON LISTENING

Real listening is rare — and how people decide if you're with them.

Active listening lights up the same brain regions as the speaker's. It changes how trusted you feel — without changing a single word you say.

🧘 CLEAR YOUR HEAD
If you're listening to confirm a bias, people see through it. Empty your agenda first.
🔀 FLIP THE SCRIPT
Move from assessment questions to curiosity questions. One feels like a test. The other opens doors.
🌊 GO DEEP
Open questions invite people to lead you to what matters most to them.

You've prepped. You've practiced. Now make the conversation revealing — and consequential.

FOUR MOVES THAT WORK

How to make sure the conversation goes somewhere.

01
🤝 SET GROUND RULES
"If something is off limits, just say so." People relax. The truth follows.
02
🎭 IMPROVISE
Walk in with twenty questions. By the fifth, you'll know which ten matter.
03
🤫 EMBRACE THE QUIET
Silence is a tool. People fill it with what they really think.
04
❓ ASK THE SIMPLE QUESTION
A clean "Why?" at the right moment outperforms five layers of context.
CHAPTER 08

Situational
fluency.

aka Adaptability — the skill that makes every other skill land.

SITUATIONAL FLUENCY

Read the room — shift in real time.

🌡️
READ THE TEMPERATURE
Diagnose the room's energy. Calibrate your tone, pace, and posture accordingly.
🪞
MATCH THEN LEAD
Mirror their energy briefly — then shift the room toward the state you need.
⚡
DROP THE SCRIPT
Rigid delivery kills connection. The best moments happen when you respond to what's actually happening.
🎛️
FLEX YOUR REGISTER
Executive with the board. Direct with your team. Warm with a customer. Fluency, not fakeness.
THE ADAPTABILITY EDGE

Why the most adaptable person wins.

🎯 CONTEXT IS EVERYTHING
A skeptic, an ally, and a decision-maker need three different approaches. One size signals low awareness.
⏱️ SPEED OF RECALIBRATION
Things go sideways. The best leaders recalibrate in seconds, not minutes. That speed earns trust.
🔄 COMFORT WITH DISCOMFORT
You can't flex if you're clinging to how you thought it would go. The pivot is where credibility is built.
